Transform Your Mind, Body & Soul in 21 Days

Join Yogam World’s live online 21-Day Yoga Workshop and build a consistent, balanced yoga practice from the comfort of your home. The program includes gentle warm-ups, gentle yoga, Surya Namaskar, asanas for digestion and detoxification, pranayama, chanting, meditation, mudras, yogic wisdom, and guidance on sattvic food. This complete journey is designed to improve flexibility, strength, breathing, relaxation, digestion, and overall well-being.
