Live Online Workshop

Transform Your Mind, Body & Soul in 21 Days

Join Yogam World’s live online 21-Day Yoga Workshop and build a consistent, balanced yoga practice from the comfort of your home. The program includes gentle warm-ups, gentle yoga, Surya Namaskar, asanas for digestion and detoxification, pranayama, chanting, meditation, mudras, yogic wisdom, and guidance on sattvic food. This complete journey is designed to improve flexibility, strength, breathing, relaxation, digestion, and overall well-being.

Meet Your Guide

Durga Devi Penke

Founder of Yogam World | Yoga Alliance Certified Teacher (RYT 200 & RYT 300) | Yoga Therapist | Meditation Teacher | NLP Practitioner

With over 14 years of teaching experience, I have guided thousands of students through yoga, meditation, and holistic wellness. My passion is helping people improve their physical health, mental well-being, and inner peace through authentic yogic practices. Through Yogam World, I offer online and in-person yoga classes, meditation programs, workshops, and my signature 21-Day Online Yoga Workshop, combining gentle yoga, pranayama, yoga nidra, mindfulness, sattvic lifestyle guidance, and holistic wellness practices. My mission is to empower individuals to live healthier, more balanced, and conscious lives.
